Methodology & caveats (for the partner conversation):
• Aggregate & anonymized — no personal data is shared with partners in this view; individual-level lead sharing would require user consent, a DPA, and (under CCPA/CPRA) may count as a "sale/share" requiring opt-out. Not legal advice.
• Net-new detection — distinguishing a brand-new signup from an existing customer reconnecting requires partner cooperation or referral tagging; this prototype treats each connect as a lead for illustration.
• Attribution — production would use referral parameters (UTM / OAuth referral fields) to defensibly claim credit and address incrementality.
